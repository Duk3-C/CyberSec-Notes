## Explain Digital Forensic Concepts
---
Digial Forensics Process:
		Sometimes data acquisition will result in chages to evidence but will still be admissible so
		long as it occurred under the proper circumstance. For example, performing forensic
		investigation on a mobile phone or tablet will require inserting a cable into the device, which
		will result in a state change on the device.
		
A forensic investigation includes the following four phases:
		1. Identification:
				- Ensure that the scene is safe. Threat to life or injury takes precedence over evidence
				collection.
				- Secure the scene to prevent contamination of evidence. Record the scene using video and
				identify witnesses for interview.
				- Identify the scope of evidence to be collected.
		2. Collection:
				- Ensure authorization to collect the evidence using tools and methods that will withstand
				legal scrutiny
				- Document and prove the integrity of evidence as it is collected and ensure that it is
				stored in secure, tamper-evident packaging.
		3. Analysis:
				- Create a copy of evidence for analysis, ensuring that the copy can be related directly to
				the primary evidence source. The integrity of evidence copies are verified by generating
				hashes of the files on a recurring basis in order to detect any unintended changes.
				- Analyze evidence using tools which are known to produce trustworthy and legally defensible
				results.
		4. Reporting/Presentation:
				Create a report of the methods and tools used and present findings and conclusions in
				accordance to the specific reporting requirements necessary.
