**Partially-known environment**:
    In penetration testing, a partially-known environment (gray-box environment) means the tester has some knowledge of the target (e.g., network diagrams, API documentation) but not complete details. This balances realism and efficiency, allowing focused testing without the full insider view of a white-box text.

Passive recon is an information-gathering technique (using public sources).

A **Tabletop Exercise** is a discussion-based session where the incident response team walks through simulated incident scenarios to validate their response plan, identify gaps, and clarify roles and responsabilities. Unlike live drill or penetration tests, tabletop exercises do not involve actual system testing, making them a low-cost, low-risk way to improve incident preparedness.

**Endpoint Detection and Response (EDR)** solutions provide continuous monitoring of endpoint activities, behavioral analysis to detect suspicious patterns, and automated response capabilities such as isolating infected endpoints. EDR goes beyond traditional signature-based antivirus by using behavioral analytics and machine learning to detect advanced threats that evade traditional detection methods.

**Change Management** is the formal process for requesting, assessing, authorizing, shceduling, implementing, validating, and documenting production changes. The appropriate change authority depends on the organization's process; higher-risk changes also need testing and a workable backout plan.


While a non-disclosure agreement (NDA) holds importance in protecting sensitive information, it is not the primary purpose of the SLA inclusion in the contract.

### SLA (Security Level Agreement)
A contract that outlines the services, performance metrics, and responsibilities of security providers and customers. It ensures clear expectations and accountability regarding security measures and compliance.

A cloud service provider actively includes an SLA to define the level of service it must deliver, covering performance metrics, reponse times, and availability.
An SLA's primary purpose is not to outline the intentions and expectations of parties in a partnership. This is the role of a Memorandum of Understanding (MOU).

**Continuous adaptive risk management**; a core zero trust concept where access deicisions are made dynamically based on multiple factors including device posture, user identity, and contextual risk.

**Password Spraying** is an attack technique that tries a small number of commonly used passwords against many accounts simultaneously. Unlike traditional brute force, password spraying avoids account lockout policies by spreading attempts accross many accounts.

A **Deauthentication attack** exploits the unprotected 802.11 management frames by sending forged deauthentication packets to wireless clients. This forces the client to disconnect and reconnect, allowing the attacker to capture the WPA/WPA2 four-way handshake during reconnection. The capture handshake can then be cracked iffline. WPA3 and 802.11w (Protected Management Frames) provide defenses against this attack.

**The Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard (PCI DSS)** is required for all organizations that store, process, or transmit credit card data. PCI DSS establishes requirements for network security, data protection, vulnerability management, access control, monitoring, and security policies to protect cardholder data from theft and fraud.
