# Data Types
---
**Regulated Data**: 
    refers to specific categories of information subject to legal or regulatory requirements regarding their handling, storage, and protection. Typically includes sensitive or personally identifiable information (PII) protected by laws and regulations to ensure privacy, security, and appropriate use.

**Trade Secrets**:
    refers to valuable, confidential information that gives a business a competitive advantage. Trade secrets encompass much nonpublic, proprietary information, including formulas, processes, methods, techniques, customer lists, pricing information, marketing strategies, and other business-critical data.

**Legal and Financial Data**:
    critical data for legal compliance, financial reporting, decision making, and risk management. Legal Data includes documents, contracts, legal agreements, court records, litigation information, intellectual property fillings, regulatory filing, and other legal documents. Financial data pertains to information concerning an organization's financial activities, performance, and transactions, including financial projections, budgets, and other financial reports.

**Human-Readable and Non-Human-Readable Data**:
    refers to information that humans can easily understand and interpret without additional processing or translation. Human-readable data describes a format that is accessible and readable, such as text, images, or multimedia content. Non-Human-Readable data refers to data that is not easily understood or interpreted by humans in its raw form. It may be in a machine-readable format, such as binary code, encrypted data, or data decipher and interpret. This kind of data often requires additional processing or transformation to make it understandable to humans.
